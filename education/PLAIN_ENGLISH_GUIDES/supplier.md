# Supplier Management in Plain English

## The Big Idea
Suppliers are extensions of your company. Choose carefully, manage actively.

## Key Concepts

### AHP
Compare criteria in pairs: "Is price more important than quality?" AHP converts judgments to weights.

### TOPSIS
Rank suppliers by closeness to ideal. Best = closest to ideal, farthest from worst.

### Kraljic Matrix
- Strategic (high risk, high profit): partner closely
- Leverage (low risk, high profit): negotiate hard
- Bottleneck (high risk, low profit): secure supply
- Routine (low risk, low profit): automate
