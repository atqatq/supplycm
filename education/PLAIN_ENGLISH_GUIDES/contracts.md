# Contracts in Plain English

## The Big Idea
Contracts determine who bears risk. The right contract aligns incentives so both profit together.

## Key Concepts

### Revenue Sharing
Supplier charges lower wholesale but gets cut of sales. Both want high sales.

### Buyback
Supplier buys back unsold goods. Retailer orders more (less risk of being stuck).

### Quantity Flexibility
Retailer can adjust order within a range. Useful when demand is uncertain.

### Double Marginalization
Both add their own markup = price too high = total profit lower. Contracts fix this.

## Memory Aid: SHARE
- Synchronize incentives
- Handle uncertainty
- Allocate risk
- Reward cooperation
- Equalize information
