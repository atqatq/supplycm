# S&OP in Plain English

## The Big Idea
Monthly meeting where sales, production, and finance agree on one plan. Prevents departmental silos.

## Key Concepts

### Chase Strategy
Production matches demand exactly. Low inventory but workforce fluctuates.

### Level Strategy
Constant production. Inventory goes up when demand is low, down when high. Stable workforce.

### Demand-Supply Match
Compare what customers want with what you can make. The gap tells you what to fix.
