# Inventory in Plain English

## The Big Idea
Balance: too much = waste, too little = lost sales. Find the "just right" amount.

## Key Concepts

### EOQ (Economic Order Quantity)
The sweet spot order size. Order too much = high holding cost. Order too little = high ordering cost. EOQ finds the balance.

### Safety Stock
Your "just in case" buffer. More uncertainty = more safety stock.

### Reorder Point
Inventory level that triggers a new order. Equals expected sales during lead time plus safety stock.

### ABC Analysis
Top 20% of items (Class A) = 80% of value. Focus on A items.

### Newsvendor Model
For perishables. Buy before knowing demand. Too many spoil, too few lose sales.
