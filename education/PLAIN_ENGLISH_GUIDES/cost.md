# Cost Analysis in Plain English

## The Big Idea
The price you pay is just one cost. Total cost includes freight, duty, insurance, holding, quality.

## Key Concepts

### Landed Cost
What a product really costs at your door: purchase + freight + duty + insurance + handling.

### TCO (Total Cost of Ownership)
Landed cost + operating + maintenance + disposal. Full lifecycle cost.

### Holding Cost
Cost of keeping inventory: capital, storage, insurance, obsolescence. Typically 20-30% of value/year.

## Memory Aid: TOTAL
- Transport (freight)
- Ordering (administrative)
- Taxes and duties
- Actual purchase price
- Loss (obsolescence, damage)
