# Sustainability in Plain English

## The Big Idea
Meet today's needs without compromising future generations. Reduce environmental impact.

## Key Concepts

### Carbon Footprint
How much CO2 your operations produce. Transport = distance * weight * emission factor.

### Reverse Logistics
Moving goods backward (returns, recycling). Traditional goes forward, reverse handles returns.

### Green Landed Cost
Include environmental costs (carbon tax, disposal) in total cost.

## Memory Aid: GREEN
- Greenhouse gas reduction
- Recycling and reuse
- Energy efficiency
- Ethical sourcing
- Nature preservation
