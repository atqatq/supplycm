# Quality in Plain English

## The Big Idea
Consistently meet customer expectations. Six Sigma aims for 3.4 defects per million.

## Key Concepts

### Control Charts
Heartbeat monitor for your process. Within limits = healthy. Outside = problem.

### Cp and Cpk
Cp: is your spread narrow enough? Cpk: is it narrow AND centered? Cpk is always less than or equal to Cp.

### DPMO
Defects Per Million Opportunities. Six Sigma = 3.4 DPMO. Most companies = 3 Sigma (66,800 DPMO).

## Memory Aid: SIX
- Define the problem
- analyze current state
- Measure key metrics
- Improve the process
- execute and control (sustain)
